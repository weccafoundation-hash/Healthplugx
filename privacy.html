Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

HealthPlugX ("we", "our", or "us") operates a healthcare technology marketplace. We connect users with independent third-party healthcare providers. We do not provide medical care.

Information we collect

Account details, booking history, device information, and communications you send to us. Payment data is processed by Paystack; we do not store full card numbers.

How we use data

To operate the marketplace, facilitate bookings between you and providers, improve our platform, send transactional notifications (email, SMS, WhatsApp), and comply with applicable law.

Sharing

We share necessary booking information with the healthcare provider you select. We do not sell personal data.
