About HealthPlugX

Connecting You to Trusted Healthcare

HealthPlugX is a healthcare technology marketplace. We help patients, corporate organisations, and verified third-party providers discover, compare, book, and access healthcare services across Nigeria and Africa.

We are not a hospital, clinic, pharmacy, laboratory, or telemedicine company. Clinical care is always delivered by independent providers listed on our platform.

Mission

To make quality healthcare services easy to discover, compare, book and access through technology.

Vision

To become Africa's most trusted healthcare marketplace connecting patients, healthcare providers and corporate organizations.
